# CHUKA UNIVERSITY CISCO Network Academy

## Learning Management System (LMS) Training Manual

---

### 1. Introduction

Welcome to the official Learning Management System of **CHUKA UNIVERSITY CISCO Network Academy**. This platform is designed to streamline your learning experience, from tracking attendance and submitting assignments to managing your academic progress and fee statements.

### 2. Getting Started: Accessing the System

- **Login Portal**: Access the LMS via your designated URL.
- **Credentials**: Use your university-provided **Username** and **Password**.
- **Security Features**:
  - **Password Toggle**: Click the "Eye" icon in the password field to see what you are typing.
  - **Remember Me**: Check this box on your personal device to stay logged in for convenience.
  - **Password Reset**: If you forget your password, use the "Forgot your password?" link. Reset requests require Admin approval for security.

---

### 3. Student Guide: Managing Your Studies

#### A. Your Dashboard

The Student Dashboard is your central hub for tracking performance.

- **Overall Average**: Displays your real-time cumulative grade across all assignments.
- **Academic Status**: Automatically shows **PASS** (50%+) or **FAIL** based on your current performance.
- **Your Cohorts**: Lists the specific intakes you are enrolled in (e.g., **CCNA 1**, **CCNA 2**, **CCNA 3**).

#### B. Attendance Tracking

Attendance is tracked automatically with a strict punctuality rule:

- **Auto-Tracking**: The system records your attendance as soon as you log in and view your dashboard while a class is live.
- **The 30-Minute Rule**: To be marked as **Present**, you must join or log in within the **first 30 minutes** of the class start time.
- **Late Joins**: Logging in after 30 minutes will record you as having joined, but you will not receive attendance marks for that session.

#### C. Assignments & Submissions

- **Available Tasks**: New assignments appear under "Available Assignments" with clear deadlines.
- **Submitting Work**: Upload your files directly through the dashboard. Once submitted, you can track the status (Pending vs. Graded).
- **Academic History**: Review past submissions, marks awarded, and detailed instructor feedback.

#### D. Fees & Payments

- **View Balances**: Check your total course fees, payments made, and current outstanding balances.
- **Reporting Payments**: Submit details for M-Pesa or Bank transactions. Admins will verify these to update your ledger.

#### E. Credentials & Recognition

- **Transcripts**: Download a PDF of your official transcript at any time to see detailed grades.
- **Certificates**: Once you successfully pass a cohort and the study period concludes, your official certificate becomes available for download.

---

### 4. Lecturer Guide: Managing Courses & Assessments

#### A. Dashboard Overview

Lecturers manage assigned cohorts, create content, and grade submissions from a unified interface.

- **Meeting Management**: Create and schedule online meetings. The system generates links for students to join.
- **Attendance Reports**: generate instant, optimized attendance reports for any assigned cohort to monitor participation.

#### B. Assignments & Grading

- **Creating Tasks**: Create assignments for specific cohorts. Note that all assignments must be **Approved by an Admin** before becoming visible to students.
- **Submitting Grades**: Review student files, award marks, and provide constructive feedback. Once a grade is approved by an Admin, it is locked and visible to the student.

---

### 5. Administrator Guide: System Oversight

#### A. User & Cohort Management

- **Users**: Admins can create, update, or delete users (Students, Lecturers, and other Admins).
- **Cohorts**: Manage the lifecycles of CCNA intakes, setting start/end dates and assigning lecturers.

#### B. Approvals & Verification

- **Internal Checks**: Admins approve or reject new assignments and lecturer-submitted grades to ensure academic integrity.
- **Payment Verification**: Verify student payment transactions (M-Pesa/Bank) before they reflect on the student's fee statement.
- **Password Resets**: Review and approve student password reset requests.

---

### 6. System Performance & Best Practices

- **Optimized Loading**: The system uses high-level compression (GZip) and database indexing to ensure that even large reports load instantly.
- **Caching**: Frequently viewed pages are cached to save time and bandwidth.
- **Mobile Friendly**: The dashboard is designed to be fully responsive on smartphones and tablets.
